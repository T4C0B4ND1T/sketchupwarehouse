A clean, modern wall-hung bathroom sink, ready to drop onto a bathroom wall. Sinks and other plumbing fixtures come up constantly in bathroom layouts, and this one stays light, so it works for floor plans, interior elevations and quick renders.

## What's in the model

- **Basin:** a white vitreous china basin, 24 in wide, 18 in deep and 6 in tall. The rectangular bowl tapers from 19 × 12 in at the rim to 16 × 9 in at the bottom and is 4½ in deep, with a 4 in deck at the back for the faucet.
- **Faucet:** a single-hole, single-lever chrome faucet, about 7 in tall, with a 5 in spout that reaches over the bowl.
- **Drain and trap:** a chrome drain flange, an 8 in tailpiece and a trap arm that runs back to a wall escutcheon.

The rim sits at 34 in above the floor, the maximum for an accessible lavatory. Every part is a solid group, and the basin, faucet and drain are grouped separately so you can swap or hide them.

## How to use it

1. Download the .skp file, then use **File → Import** to add it to your model.
2. The component origin is at floor level at the front-left corner, and the back of the sink is the wall side. Set it on the floor against a wall and the rim lands at 34 in.
3. To match a different finish, open the component and repaint the Faucet or Drain_and_Trap groups.
