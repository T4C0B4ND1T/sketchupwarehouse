A clean, low-poly sedan for dropping into driveways, parking lots and street scenes. Cars are among the most searched models on 3D Warehouse. This one is kept deliberately simple, so you can place dozens of them without slowing your model down.

## What's in the model

- **Body:** a blue body with a sloped hood and trunk and wheel arches cut into the sides. It's 192 in (16 ft) long and 72 in wide, about the size of a typical midsize sedan.
- **Cabin:** dark glass with a body-colored roof and center pillar. The roof is 57 in off the ground.
- **Wheels:** four wheels with black tires and silver rims, 27 in in diameter, on a 111 in wheelbase. They share one component, so editing one updates all four.
- **Details:** headlights, red taillights and two side mirrors, all built as components.

Every part is a solid group, about 330 faces in all.

## How to use it

1. Download the .skp file, then use **File → Import** to add it to your model.
2. The component origin is at ground level at the rear-left corner, and the car faces +X (the red axis). Rotate it to line up with a parking stall or lane.
3. To change the color, open the component and repaint the Body, Roof and B_Pillar groups and the mirror component.
