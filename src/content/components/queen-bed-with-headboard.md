A queen platform bed, dressed and ready to drop into a bedroom layout. Beds are among the top searches on 3D Warehouse. This one stays simple and light, so it works for space planning, interior elevations and quick renders.

## What's in the model

- **Platform frame:** a walnut-colored platform, 63 × 83 in and 8 in deep, on four 2½ in square legs. The top of the platform is 12 in off the floor.
- **Headboard:** a grey upholstered panel, 64 in wide, 3 in thick and 50 in tall, standing on the floor behind the platform.
- **Mattress:** a standard 60 × 80 in queen mattress, 10 in thick, so the sleeping surface is 22 in high.
- **Bedding:** a slate-blue duvet over the lower three-quarters of the mattress, a turned-down white sheet, and two 26 × 19 in pillows.

Every part is a solid group. The legs and pillows are components, so editing one updates its copies.

## How to use it

1. Download the .skp file, then use **File → Import** to add it to your model.
2. The component origin is the front-left corner of the frame at floor level, and the headboard is at the back. Push the headboard against a wall and you're done.
3. To match your scheme, open the component and repaint the Duvet, Headboard or Platform groups.
