A standard 36 × 60 in double-hung window, one of the most common residential window sizes. It's built as a SketchUp component, so you can place many copies and edit them all at once.

## What's in the model

- **Frame:** a head, two jambs and a sill. Each part is 1.5 in wide and 4.5 in deep. The sill projects 1 in past the exterior face.
- **Two sashes:** each sash is 33 in wide and 29¼ in tall and sits in its own track. The upper sash sits toward the exterior and the lower sash sits toward the interior. They overlap by 1½ in at the meeting rails, as they would in a real double-hung window.
- **Glass:** a ¼ in clear pane in each sash, using a semi-transparent material.

Every frame and sash part is a separate solid group. The two sashes are instances of one sash component, so editing one changes both.

## How to use it

1. Download the .skp file, then use **File → Import** to add it to your model.
2. Place the window in a wall opening 36 in wide and 60 in tall. The component origin is the exterior bottom-left corner of the frame, and the sill extends 1 in outward from there.
3. To change the color, open the component and use the **Paint Bucket** on the frame parts. Because the sashes share one definition, painting one sash also paints the other.
