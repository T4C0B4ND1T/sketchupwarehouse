Trimble is selling most SketchUp subscriptions at 25% off through December 4, 2026. On Trimble's own pricing page that means Pro drops from $399 to $299.25 per user per year, and Studio from $859 to $644.25, using the code CYBERSK26. It's a one-term discount on new purchases and some upgrades, not on renewals, so check which bucket you're in before you spend anything.

## What's on offer

Trimble's pricing page lists the following, all per user per year in USD:

| Plan | Regular price | With CYBERSK26 |
|---|---|---|
| Go | $129 | No discount listed |
| Pro | $399 | $299.25 |
| Studio | $859 | $644.25 |
| Pro Scan | $499 | $374.25 |
| Pro Civil Contractor | $499 | $374.25 |
| Pro Advanced Workflows | $599 | $449.25 |

The page says the discount applies to "select SketchUp plans" and lists Pro, Studio, Pro Scan, Pro Civil Contractor and Pro Advanced Workflows. It shows no discount for Go, education or team plans. Prices exclude tax and VAT. Education plans are listed at $55 per user per year, and team pricing goes through sales.

Prices and terms change, so treat the table as a snapshot of the page when I read it and confirm at checkout.

### Dates and resellers

Trimble's page gives November 23 to December 4. Resellers Softprom and Design8 both advertise 20 November to 4 December 2026, and Design8 says the promotion can't be used from 5 December. If you buy through a reseller, the discount is applied through them rather than with a code on Trimble's site. Softprom's page lists no code and routes orders through a contact form.

Reseller terms also list a few things Trimble's page doesn't spell out. Softprom includes Go and Sefaira among eligible products. Trimble's page doesn't list a Go discount. If you're a Go buyer, ask the seller to confirm in writing before paying.

## Who qualifies

Per the reseller pages (Softprom and Design8 describe the same structure):

- **New customers** can buy annual subscriptions at the discount, with no cap on seats per order.
- **Existing customers** can add seats at the discounted price, prorated to the current subscription's anniversary date. They can also upgrade from Pro to Studio, or from Pro to Pro Advanced Workflows, at 25% off.
- **Not covered:** renewals, education licenses and orders placed before the promotion.
- The discount applies to one subscription term only and can't be combined with other offers.

That "renewals excluded" line is the one that matters most. If your Pro seat renews in January, this sale does nothing for it.

## Why the timing is interesting

SketchUp 2027 shipped on October 21, 2026, according to the [official release notes](https://help.sketchup.com/en/release-notes/sketchup-desktop-20270). If you've been waiting for a reason to move, here's the short version of what changed:

- Models and LayOut documents can be open together in one session, with tabs and floating windows.
- Windows and macOS share the same desktop interface.
- 2027 runs only on Apple silicon Macs; Intel Macs are dropped.
- There's more control over DWG/DXF, IFC and USD exchange.

For the details, see our write-ups on [what's in SketchUp 2027](/blog/sketchup-2027-is-out-procedures-mac-rebuild-intel-dropped/) and the [file exchange changes](/blog/sketchup-2027-file-exchange-dwg-ifc-usd-and-crs-changes/).

Also note that on a subscription, the discount buys you the current subscription term; you aren't buying a perpetual 2027 license. Next year's renewal is at whatever the list price is then.

### Worth it?

- **Time saved:** None directly. The value is in the price.
- **Cost:** A $99.75 saving on Pro, $214.75 on Studio, and $149.75 on Pro Advanced Workflows, based on the listed prices.
- **Skip it if:** you're renewing, you're a student or educator, you only need Go, or you can't say what you'd do with Studio's extra features. A bigger plan you don't use is not a saving.

## Before you buy

1. **Check your hardware.** If you're on an Intel Mac, 2027 won't run. Read our [SketchUp system requirements](/blog/sketchup-system-requirements/) guide first, since buying a seat you can't launch is an expensive mistake.
2. **Check your support window.** Older versions fall out of support on a schedule. See [SketchUp 2024 end of support](/blog/sketchup-end-of-support-2024-ends-jan-31-2027-what-to-do/) if you're still on that release.
3. **Decide the tier on features, not the discount.** Pro covers LayOut and the core modeling toolset. Studio costs more because it adds more; Pro Scan, Pro Civil Contractor and Pro Advanced Workflows target specific jobs. Buy the one that matches your deliverables.
4. **Consider the renderer.** Chaos is also running a separate offer through November 30, per Design8: 20% off new and additional Enscape, V-Ray, Corona and Veras licenses, and 10% off tier upgrades within a product family. That one is a reseller's report, so confirm on Chaos's site. Don't stack purchases just because both are cheap.
5. **Buy through one channel and keep the receipt.** Trimble's code and reseller discounts are separate routes, and offers can't be combined.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming a renewal qualifies.** It doesn't, per the reseller terms.
- **Assuming the reseller dates match Trimble's.** They differ by three days at the start, so don't plan around the earliest date.
- **Buying an upgrade you can't verify.** If you're on Pro and want Studio, confirm that the Pro-to-Studio upgrade is eligible on your account before you pay.
- **Forgetting tax.** The listed prices exclude tax and VAT.

## What this means for you

- **New to SketchUp and need Pro or Studio:** Act now. This is the cheapest listed price on the page, and it ends December 4.
- **Adding seats for a team:** Act now, but get the proration in writing. Seats are prorated to your anniversary date.
- **Pro user eyeing Studio or Advanced Workflows:** Act now only if you've already decided you need it. Upgrades qualify at 25% off.
- **Renewing:** Ignore it. Renewals are excluded.
- **Go, education or team plans:** Ignore it unless your seller confirms otherwise in writing.
- **Intel Mac owner:** Hold off until your hardware is sorted. 2027 is Apple silicon only.
- **Render licenses:** Check Chaos directly before November 30 if you were already planning to buy.

## FAQ

### When does the SketchUp Cyber Week sale end?

December 4, 2026. Trimble's page lists a start of November 23; reseller pages list November 20.

### What is the SketchUp Cyber Week discount code?

Trimble's pricing page lists CYBERSK26 for 25% off select plans. Reseller pages don't list a code, and the discount is applied when you order through them.

### Does the discount apply to renewals?

No. Reseller terms exclude renewals, education licenses and orders placed before the promotion. It applies to one subscription term only.

### Is SketchUp Go discounted?

Not on Trimble's pricing page, which shows Go at $129 with no discount listed. Softprom's page includes Go among eligible products, so ask your seller to confirm before you pay.
