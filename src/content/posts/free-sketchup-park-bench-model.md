Benches are some of the most downloaded site elements on 3D Warehouse, and a good one gets placed dozens of times in a single site plan. This free 6 ft park bench is built from just three small components, so a whole promenade of them stays light. It's free for personal and commercial projects.

**Get it:** [download the .skp](/components/park-bench-6ft/) from our library, or [open it on 3D Warehouse](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/model/50b5e8ad-170d-4e81-8fc6-617844d33c52/Park-Bench-6-ft-with-Backrest) to load it straight into SketchUp.

## What's in the model

- **End frames:** two black cast-iron frames, 2½ in thick, set 6 in in from each end. Each has a front leg, a seat rail and a rear post that leans back about 15° to carry the backrest.
- **Seat:** four 72 × 3½ × 1½ in slats in a teak color, giving an 18 in seat height and about 17 in of seat depth.
- **Backrest:** three matching slats on the leaning posts. The bench stands 33 in tall overall, and the full size is 72 × 24.5 × 33 in.

Every part is a solid group.

## Using it on a site plan

1. Import the .skp with **File → Import**, or use the 3D Warehouse panel inside SketchUp.
2. The origin is at ground level at the front-left corner, and the seat faces −Y, so it sits flat on paving and is easy to rotate toward a path.
3. Copy it along a walkway with the **Move** tool and **Ctrl** (Option on Mac), then type a multiplier like `x8` to array it.
4. To recolor the wood, open the component and paint one seat slat and one back slat. Every other slat follows.
