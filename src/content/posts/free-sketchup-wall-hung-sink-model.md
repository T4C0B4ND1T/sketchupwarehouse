Bathrooms show up in almost every residential and commercial plan, and a clean sink is one of the fixtures you reach for most. This free 24 in wall-hung sink is our first plumbing model. It's simple and light, so it works in floor plans, interior elevations and quick renders. It's free for personal and commercial projects.

**Get it:** [download the .skp](/components/wall-hung-lavatory-24in/) from our library, or [open it on 3D Warehouse](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/model/2b617998-80bd-48aa-867b-e8b3c4a92e2e/Wall-Hung-Bathroom-Sink-with-Faucet-24-in) to load it straight into SketchUp.

## What's in the model

- **Basin:** a white vitreous china basin, 24 in wide, 18 in deep and 6 in tall. The rectangular bowl is 4½ in deep and tapers toward the bottom, with a 4 in deck at the back for the faucet.
- **Faucet:** a single-hole, single-lever chrome faucet with a 5 in spout that reaches over the bowl.
- **Drain and trap:** a chrome drain flange, an 8 in tailpiece and a trap arm that runs back to a wall escutcheon.

The rim sits at 34 in above the floor, the maximum height for an accessible lavatory. Every part is a solid group, and the basin, faucet and drain are grouped separately.

## Using it in a bathroom layout

1. Import the .skp with **File → Import**, or use the 3D Warehouse panel inside SketchUp.
2. The origin is at floor level at the front-left corner, and the back of the sink is the wall side. Set it on the floor against a wall and the rim lands at 34 in.
3. For an accessible layout, leave a 30 × 48 in clear floor space in front of the sink.
4. To swap the finish, open the component and repaint the Faucet or Drain_and_Trap groups.
