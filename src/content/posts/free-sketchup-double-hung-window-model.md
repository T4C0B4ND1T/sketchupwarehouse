The first model in our [free components library](/components/) is a standard 36 × 60 in double-hung window, one of the most common residential window sizes. It's free for personal and commercial projects.

**Get it:** [download the .skp](/components/double-hung-window-36x60/) from our library, or [open it on 3D Warehouse](https://3dwarehouse.sketchup.com/model/a4a429ab-da9a-4c00-8592-02759d2d841e) to load it straight into SketchUp.

## What's in the model

- **Frame:** a head, two jambs and a sill, each 1.5 in wide and 4.5 in deep. The sill projects 1 in past the exterior face.
- **Two sashes:** each is 33 in wide and 29¼ in tall and sits in its own track, overlapping 1½ in at the meeting rails like a real double-hung unit.
- **Glass:** a ¼ in clear pane in each sash, using a semi-transparent material.

Every part is a separate solid group, and both sashes are instances of one component, so editing one edits both.

## Placing it in a wall

1. Import the .skp with **File → Import**, or use the 3D Warehouse panel inside SketchUp.
2. Cut a 36 × 60 in opening in your wall. The component origin is the exterior bottom-left corner of the frame.
3. Snap that corner to the opening. The sill sticks out 1 in from the exterior face.
4. To recolor the frame, open the component and use the **Paint Bucket**. Painting one sash paints both.

Because it's a component, you can copy it into every opening on an elevation and keep the file light.
