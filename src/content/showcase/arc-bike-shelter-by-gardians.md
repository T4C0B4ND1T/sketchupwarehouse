Gardians makes covered bike parking, and this model is their Arc shelter, set up as a cycle station for both secure bike storage and bike rental. A shallow arched canopy on slim posts covers a run of bike racks, and the preview places it along a paved campus walk with autumn trees and bollards.

At around 33,000 faces it carries the canopy frame and the racks in useful detail, which makes it a good fit for streetscape and campus site plans. It has over 27,000 downloads on 3D Warehouse.
