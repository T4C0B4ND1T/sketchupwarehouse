D Johansson modeled the PH Artichoke, Poul Henningsen's well-known pendant from more than 40 years ago. As the creator's description explains, twelve steel arches carry 72 leaves in twelve circular rows of six, and each row is staggered from the one before so the leaves cover each other and hide the bulb from every angle.

The model builds out every leaf in its rows at around 9,500 faces, enough to read as the real fixture over a dining table or in a lobby render. With over 115,000 downloads, it's one of the most popular pendant lights on 3D Warehouse.
