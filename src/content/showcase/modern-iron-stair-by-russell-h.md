Russell H. built this as a modular stair, and it reads like something out of a contemporary loft. Open wood treads cantilever off a single dark steel stringer and climb to an upper landing, where a frameless glass railing runs along the edge of the floor above.

At roughly 14,000 faces it holds the stair, landing and railing together in one piece, so you can drop it into a double-height space and see the whole connection at once. With over 180,000 downloads, it's one of the most popular stairs on 3D Warehouse.
