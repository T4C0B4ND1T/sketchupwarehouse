SketchyKo Designs usually models houses and buildings, but after building a school with a playset they took to designing playgrounds, and this one strings many playsets together. Timber posts carry raised platforms with red panels and porthole cutouts, linked by a hanging plank bridge, with blue rope nets and ladders to climb and a red slide to get down.

At about 51,000 faces every rope, plank and rung is modeled, so it holds up in a close render of a park or schoolyard. With over 226,000 downloads, it's one of the most popular playgrounds on 3D Warehouse.
